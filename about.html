About The Dog Owners Hub

The Dog Owners Hub is a resource for dog owners at every stage — from the first week with a new puppy to the quiet, careful years with a senior dog. The goal is simple: practical guidance you can actually use, explained honestly, without the guesswork or scare tactics common in a lot of pet content online.

Our approach

Training and behavior content on this site is grounded in established learning theory (the science of how animals learn through reinforcement) rather than trends or opinion. Where we cover the psychology of dog behavior and quality of life, we lean on published animal welfare science — including the Five Domains Model used by welfare researchers — rather than generic advice.

About the author

This site is written by David Moreno, a licensed psychologist (Psicólogo General Sanitario, Spain) with a working background in behavior science, learning theory, and human wellbeing. That training informs how this site approaches canine behavior and quality of life: through the lens of behavior science and animal welfare research.

A note on scope, in the interest of transparency: David's license is in human psychology, not veterinary medicine or certified animal behavior consulting. Content on this site that touches behavior and psychology draws on published, citable research and general behavior-science principles — it is not a substitute for a consultation with a licensed veterinarian or a certified veterinary behaviorist, particularly for any concern involving your dog's physical health, sudden behavior changes, or aggression.

Editorial standards

Every guide is written to be genuinely useful first. Where we recommend products, we evaluate them against clear, stated criteria rather than accepting payment for placement.
